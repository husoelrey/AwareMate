# AwareMate - Roadmap

The roadmap outlines the strategic phases for developing AwareMate, evolving it from a core MVP into a comprehensive cross-platform awareness ecosystem.

## v1.0 MVP (Digital + Personal Awareness, Android)
- **Target:** Android OS
- **Features:**
  - Complete Companion system (Seed to Ancient Tree)
  - Momentum score calculation
  - Digital Awareness Module (Screen time analytics, mindful nudges, digital sunset)
  - Personal Growth Module (Mood journal, breath exercises, daily micro-challenges, reflection & self-discovery prompts)
  - Local-first architecture with Firebase sync
  - Optional donation/sponsorship link for sustainability (no ads, no paywall)

## v1.1 Environmental Awareness Module
- **Focus:** Integrating GreenComp principles.
- **Features:**
  - Green challenges and carbon footprint tips
  - Companion reactions to eco-friendly habits
  - Environmental education snippets

## v1.2 Social/Civic Module
- **Focus:** Civic participation and community engagement.
- **Features:**
  - Community action prompts
  - Civic participation tracking
  - Volunteering and local impact suggestions

## v1.3 Multi-language
- **Focus:** Expanding accessibility across Erasmus+ partner countries.
- **Features:**
  - i18n support infrastructure
  - Core languages: English (EN), Turkish (TR)
  - Community-driven translations for partner languages

## v1.4 Cross-platform
- **Focus:** Reaching all users regardless of their device.
- **Features:**
  - iOS deployment via Compose Multiplatform
  - Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux) deployment
  - Web/Wasm experimental build

## v1.5 Youthpass/DigComp Certificate Generation
- **Focus:** Formal recognition of user growth.
- **Features:**
  - Automated certificate generation detailing completed modules and skills acquired
  - Export to PDF and sharing capabilities

## v2.0 AI-powered Personalization
- **Focus:** Intelligent companion guidance.
- **Features:**
  - LLM-based smart suggestions for micro-challenges
  - Personalized reflection prompts based on journal entries (privacy-preserving)

## v2.1 Doom-scroll Interceptor
- **Focus:** Advanced digital awareness interventions.
- **Features:**
  - Android Accessibility Service integration to detect and gently interrupt doom-scrolling patterns in specific social media apps

## v2.2 Community Features
- **Focus:** Collective growth.
- **Features:**
  - Shared community challenges
  - Anonymous, opt-in leaderboards
  - Group momentum goals
