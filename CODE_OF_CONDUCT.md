# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

AwareMate is built around compassion, youth safety, accessibility, and inclusion. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and respect;
- give constructive, specific feedback;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability;
- protect personal and sensitive information;
- keep product discussions free from shame-driven, manipulative, or unsafe design proposals.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these standards. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and centered on community safety.

For ordinary project concerns, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Use GitHub's private reporting and abuse-reporting tools when a concern involves safety, privacy, or a platform policy violation. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
